# 04 — Per-share figures across a stock split, in one window

**Decide:** Found live, not by phrase coverage. `NVIDIA diluted EPS over the last 10 quarters year over year` (6 October 2026) shows the quarter ended 28 April 2024 at $5.98, its EPS as first filed before the ten-for-one split of June 2024, and the next quarter at $0.67. The chart falls 90% between them, and no note says why. Each row's year-over-year change is right on its own basis (ADR 0009), but the values are not comparable across the window.

Options:

1. **Show each quarter as the latest filing reports it** (recommended). A later 10-Q reports the earlier quarter as a comparative, restated for the split ($0.60 here). Read each per-share value from the newest filing that reports that quarter, as ADR 0009 already does for the base of a change. The window is then on one basis, and the figure links to the filing it came from.
2. **Keep the values as first filed, and say so.** A note names the split and the quarter where the basis changes. Every value then matches its own filing, but the chart still shows a cliff.

Either way, a per-share figure (`eps_diluted`, `eps_basic`, `dividends_per_share`, `price` where relevant) across a split should not read as a 90% fall. ADR 0007 covers per-share figures; record the decision there or in ADR 0009.

**Acceptance:** a window across a split shows per-share values on one basis, or says it does not; a test on recorded or fake facts across a split covers it.
